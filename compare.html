Five reasons to switch

  • No meter runningEvery row, page and export is free. There are no credits to watch.
  • Your data stays yoursPages and files are read on your computer. Nothing comes to us: DigUp has no server.
  • Pages and files, one tableSaved pages, CSV, Excel, Word, PDF and scans, or a pasted list of web addresses.
  • Clean before you saveA real spreadsheet: trim, split, change case, keep only numbers, remove duplicates, undo.
  • Honest by designDigUp never works around sign-ins, paywalls or checks that you're human. It stops and tells you.

What DigUp doesn't do

  • Check pages while Chrome is closed. Checks run in your browser, so your data never sits on someone else's server.
  • Get past sign-ins, paywalls or bot checks. That's on purpose.
  • Send anything to an AI in the cloud, unless you add your own Claude key.
