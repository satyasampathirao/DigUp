Common problems, quick fixes

It didn't find the list I wanted

Open DigUp on the page, press Pick on page and click any item of the list you want. If the page was still loading, press Scan again.

I wanted every email, link or image on the page

Under the lists DigUp found, Everything on this page gives you all the emails, links, images or phone numbers on the page in one click, even when there is no list.

It only got the first page

In More pages, choose Next button (DigUp suggests one) or Infinite scroll, then press Start. Chrome asks once for access to the site.

The page asked me to sign in or prove I'm human

DigUp never gets past sign-ins or bot checks. Do it yourself in the tab, then press Scan again in DigUp.

I wasn't sure what it does with my data

Nothing leaves your browser unless you send it to a Google Sheet or a webhook you chose. No account, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
