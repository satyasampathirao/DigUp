Questions

Is DigUp really free?

Yes. No account, no credits and no row limits. Everything runs on your computer, so there are no servers to pay for.

Does DigUp send my data anywhere?

No. Pages are read in your browser and nothing is sent to the developer. Rows leave your browser only when you send them to a Google Sheet or a webhook you chose. See the privacy policy.

Which websites does it work on?

Most pages with a table or a repeating list: shops, directories, job boards, search results and more. Browser pages and the Chrome Web Store are off-limits to all extensions. Please respect each website's terms of use.

What about CAPTCHAs and sign-in pages?

DigUp never gets past them. If a page asks you to verify or sign in, do it yourself in the tab and press Scan again.

Do page checks run when Chrome is closed?

No. Checks run while Chrome is open, and a missed check runs when Chrome starts again. That is the price of keeping everything on your device.

How do I report a problem or ask for a feature?

Use "Report a problem" in DigUp's menu, the feedback form, or email digup@healthyailabs.com.
